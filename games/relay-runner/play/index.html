Relay Runner

The tactical platformer formerly developed as Pixel Patrol and Kernel Courier finally has its canonical identity. Two real missions. One standardized Signal Baton.

01 · DEAD DROP RIDGE

Sync 3 relays, recover up to 4 satchel charges, then destroy the 100-HP jammer generator.

02 · BROKEN NET VALLEY

Break fixed firewall barriers, tag three isolated evacuation teams, then reach extraction.

Move / Jump
A/D or arrows move. Space jumps. Mobile has dedicated buttons.
Signal Baton / Context
W/Up or ACTION syncs relays, tags teams, collects/plants charges, or swings the baton.
Signal Slam
S/Down while airborne slams downward and damages enemies, firewalls, or the jammer.
Arcade-True
Both maps, objectives, barriers, enemies, charges, teams, and finale locations are fixed every run.
