Drive
D / Right = gas. A / Left = reverse. Mobile uses GAS / REVERSE.
Pitch
W / Up = nose up. S / Down = nose down. Works hardest in the air.
Boost
Space / Shift or BOOST. Recharges automatically when released.
Recover
R or the center button puts the truck upright near its current position. Arena damage stays.
