# Monster Truck Mayhem — Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-30

Canonical KGBA public release.

### Historical foundation
The original July 26, 2026 Monster Truck Mayhem prototype was explicitly designed as:
- no points,
- no objective,
- pure monster-truck mayhem,
- big jumps,
- flips,
- car crushing.

The last recorded iteration that day enlarged the launch ramps and retuned the suspension toward:
- spring/damper wheel response,
- improved wheel-ground contact,
- chassis weight transfer,
- progressive compression/rebound,
- stronger landing response,
- tuned air control.

### Public v1
- Preserves the no-score / no-mission / no-timer / no-fail-state identity.
- One fixed 5,200-unit stunt arena.
- Fixed car stacks, ramps, tabletop, mega ramp, roller yard, and final launcher.
- Crushable parked cars deform and stay crushed until full arena reset.
- Suspension uses per-wheel spring/damper contact forces and chassis torque.
- Gas, reverse, pitch, boost, and recover controls on desktop and mobile.
- Session counters show crushes, completed full rotations, and airtime as descriptive stats only—not points or objectives.
- `Recover Truck` rights the truck without resetting arena damage.
- `Reset Arena` restores the entire fixed sandbox.
- Persistent sound preference and background pause.
- PWA packaging.

### Arcade-True interpretation
Monster Truck Mayhem is a mastery sandbox rather than a score-chase game.
The arena does not randomize. Players learn the same ramp geometry and landing zones repeatedly and become better at controlling the truck.
