Monster Truck Mayhem

No points. No timer. No mission. No finish line. One fixed stunt arena, oversized ramps, crushable cars, tuned suspension, flips, boost, and as much dumb monster-truck nonsense as you can manufacture.

▶ CAUSE PROBLEMSGame Vault
Arcade-True without a score: the arena never rerolls. Learn every ramp, landing, car stack, and recovery line. Mastery is measured by what you can make the truck do—not by a number we invented.

DRIVE IT

Throttle, reverse, brake, and boost across a fixed dirt arena. The suspension loads, compresses, rebounds, and transfers weight through landings.

THROW IT

Use pitch control in the air to save landings, finish rotations, or turn a questionable decision into a much worse decision.

CRUSH IT

Parked cars deform under wheel and chassis impacts. Reset the truck whenever you want—or reset the whole arena and do it again cleaner.
