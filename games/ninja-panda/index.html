Ninja Panda

DOJO HUNT. He runs the floor, climbs the middle line, rides the zipline, hits the roof, peeks from crates, flips, slides, and refuses to leave the screen. Learn the route. Land one hit. Survive the fightback. Then click him again before the smoke bomb saves him.

▶ FIND THE PANDAGame Vault
Arcade-True: Panda's dojo route is an authored loop. The same movement beats repeat in the same order, but cursor/touch proximity can make him hop away from you. You learn the route; he reacts to you.

WATCH, DON'T CHASE

Low run, box peek, middle-platform jump, zipline, rooftop run, flip, slide. His route loops in a fixed order. Learn where he will be.

TWO-CLICK COMBAT

The first clean hit triggers fightback and opens a short capture window. The second clean hit captures him. Waste it and he smoke-bombs out.

MISSES HAVE TEETH

Click empty space and Panda throws a shuriken back at your reticle, costing precious clock. Wild tapping is worse than patience.
