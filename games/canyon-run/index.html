Canyon Run

The jet never stops moving. Push left and you move left. Push right and you move right. Up climbs. Down descends. Release the control and the aircraft levels itself instead of making you undo some invisible flight-control state.

▶ ENTER THE CANYONGame Vault
Arcade flight, not pilot training: one fixed canyon route, fixed gates and hazards, obvious screen-space movement, automatic pitch/roll centering, and a brake that buys thinking time without stopping forward flight.

MOVE WHERE YOU MEAN

Left/right translate across the canyon while the jet banks visually. Up/down change altitude while the nose pitches. Let go and the attitude returns to neutral.

LEARN THE ROUTE

The canyon bends, squeezes, rises, drops, and places gates and rock hazards at the same distances every run. Failed flights become route knowledge.

BRAKE, DON'T STOP

Brake reduces forward speed enough to read a bad section or line up a gate, but the aircraft always keeps moving. The canyon is still coming.
