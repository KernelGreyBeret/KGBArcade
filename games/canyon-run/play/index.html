Canyon Run

This is an arcade flyer. You are not trimming an aircraft. You are telling a jet where on the screen you want it to go.

Left / Right
A/D or arrows move laterally. The jet banks while held, then automatically levels when released.
Up / Down
W/S or arrows climb/descend. The nose pitches while held, then automatically levels when released.
Brake
Space / Shift or BRAKE slows forward motion without stopping it. Use it to buy reaction time.
The Route
4,800 meters of fixed canyon geometry, gates, arches, rock spires, and squeeze sections. Don't crash.
ARCADE-TRUE: every bend and obstacle is authored. If you die at 2,300 meters, the answer is to remember 2,300 meters—not hope the canyon rolls something easier next run.
