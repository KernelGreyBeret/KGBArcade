Jungle Breakout

One motorcycle. One authored 1,180-meter jungle course. Mud, vines, logs, collapsing trail, ravines, monkeys, and a jaguar pack that never quits. Mudline Run is the same course every time—survival turns failure into route knowledge.

▶ RIDE OUTGame Vault
Arcade-True: Mudline Run is fixed and learnable. Hazards do not reroll after a loss. If the creek caught you at 530 meters, the creek will still be waiting at 530 meters next run.

READ THE TRAIL

Three lanes are not always three lanes. Watch the dirt, commit early, and remember where the course collapses into single-lane chokes.

RIDE THE BIKE

Steer, jump, duck, brake, and boost. Each control solves a different problem—and using the right move at the wrong time can feed the pack.

BEAT THE PACK

The jaguar gap is your living pressure meter. Mud, crashes, braking, and bad lines surrender ground. Clean riding and boost buy it back.
