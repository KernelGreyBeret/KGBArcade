Kernel Courier Fresh Corn Run

Twelve ears of fresh corn. One tiny farm truck. One fixed country route. Keep the fuel up, keep the cargo in the bed, honk the chickens out of the road, and get all twelve to the barn before the clock makes you tomorrow's problem.

▶ MAKE THE DELIVERYGame Vault
Arcade-True delivery: every mud patch, rock, chicken crossing, hay bale, broken fence, fuel can, boost pickup, and extra-corn recovery appears in the same place every run. You learn the route like a delivery driver should.

PROTECT THE LOAD

You start with exactly 12 corn. Hard impacts can knock cargo loose. Extra-corn pickups are your only way to recover a loss before delivery.

HONK WITH PURPOSE

Chicken crossings are predictable. Hit HORN before you reach them and they scatter. Ignore them and the collision costs time, damage, and possibly cargo.

MANAGE THE RUN

Boost saves time but burns more fuel. Fuel cans and boost pickups are fixed. The best route is not always maximum throttle.
