Cluck & Cover

You're the rooster. Those are your chicks. Everything with teeth, claws, or terrible intentions is about to have a very bad day. Peck fast, spur hard, flap predators away from the flock, and survive until sunset.

Arcade-True farm chaos: one fixed farm day, fixed chick paths, fixed predator entrances, fixed wave order. By the third run you should know exactly when the snake problem becomes a raccoon problem—and when the sky becomes your problem.

PECK

Fast, short-range jab. Great for snakes and finishing anything already in your face. Low commitment means you can keep repositioning.

SPUR

Heavy forward strike. Slower, stronger, and ideal for raccoons or anything stubborn enough to stay on the ground.

FLAP

Wide knockback burst and your best anti-air answer. Hawks hate it. Chicks appreciate it. Feathers happen.
