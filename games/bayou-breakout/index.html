Bayou Breakout

WARM-UP RUN. Thread a little boat through one winding swamp course while the gator pack closes behind you. Rocks, logs, sandbars, downed trees, and new gators on the banks are all exactly where you left them last run.

▶ HIT THE WATERGame Vault
Arcade-True: the river, banks, hazards, joining gators, and extraction point are authored and repeatable. A failed run is a map of what to do differently next time.

READ THE RIVER

The waterway bends and narrows through a fixed route. Learn the banks early enough that you are steering into open water instead of reacting after it disappears.

MANAGE THE GAP

Clean lines preserve your lead. Muddy banks and collisions surrender it. Boost buys distance, but the tank has to recharge.

WATCH THE SHORE

Some gators are scenery. Some join the chase at fixed points as you pass. By run three, that shouldn't be a surprise anymore.
